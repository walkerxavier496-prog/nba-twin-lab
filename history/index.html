06 / METHOD & COVERAGE

Exactly what was tested

Transparent enough to reproduce, and explicit about what is missing.

Walk-forward reconstruction

Each forecast sees only finals timestamped before that game. The 2020–21 season warms up Elo; 6,150 games from the next five seasons are evaluated.

Previous-season profiles only

Efficiency, pace, shooting, turnovers and rebounding come only from the prior completed season. Same-season full-year summaries are never used to predict earlier games.

Locked optimisation

The blend and calibration were chosen on three training seasons. One validation season checks stability; the final season stays untouched until scoring.

Uncertainty, not victory laps

Paired game bootstrap intervals measure whether differences are distinguishable from noise. A tiny point estimate is not called an improvement when its interval crosses zero.
