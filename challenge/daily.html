How to interpret the challenge

M1 is the existing production Elo baseline. M2 uses basketball science, M3 inferred player impact and rotation, M4 a possession Monte Carlo, M5 regularised machine learning and M6 an earlier-season-fitted ensemble. A missing or late forecast is excluded rather than fabricated. Brier and log loss matter more than raw winner-pick percentage when judging whether a model's probabilities are reliable.
