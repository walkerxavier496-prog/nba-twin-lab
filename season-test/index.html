How to read the table

  • Model # ranks teams by expected winning percentage in the games tested.
  • Expected W is the sum of win probabilities, not a count of picks.
  • Picked W–L assumes every game goes to the model's pregame favourite.
  • Actual W–L is what really happened.
  • Δ W is actual wins minus expected wins; positive means the team outperformed our model.
  • Open 82 / Now 82 are projected final regular-season wins based on opening-night and updated strength, respectively. They use an equally weighted opponent pool, not the exact remaining schedule.

What the model knows

  • 2025–26 regular-season Elo and team SRS set the opening strength.
  • Verified 2026 preseason wins and losses receive roughly 11% of a normal game's Elo update and fade over the first few games.
  • Regular-season games update team strength after the final buzzer. Previous-season SRS influence fades as trends develop.
  • Home court, rest and previous scoring rates are considered.
  • It does not know injuries, lineups, trades or bookmaker odds. Early-season projections are uncertain.
