01 / SCIENCE OF THE GAME

Which basketball signals improve predictions?

Opponent-adjusted strength, efficiency momentum, shooting style, turnover pressure, offensive rebounding, free-throw tendencies, rest, pace and prior head-to-head meetings. Head-to-head history is a testable signal—not a special rivalry bonus.
