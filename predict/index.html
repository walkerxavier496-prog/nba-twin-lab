03 / TRANSPARENCY

How this model works

Explainable estimates, not invented certainty.

01

Opponent-adjusted team strength

Elo moves after every final score. Beating a strong opponent counts more than beating a weak one; point margin adjusts the update. Ratings regress toward average between seasons.

02

Matchup & schedule context

The prediction combines the two teams' Elo ratings, home-court advantage, back-to-back rest, and shrinkage-adjusted points scored and allowed to estimate a winner and score.

03

Real-world verification

Walk-forward evaluation recreates the forecast before each finished game using only earlier results. Accuracy, Brier score and projected-score error update when new games finish.

04

Limits we won't hide

Injuries, confirmed lineups, possession-based team ratings, shot profiles, trades and betting odds are not yet included. A 65% prediction means a model estimate, not a guaranteed result.

Data provenance: Public, unofficial ESPN NBA scoreboard. This site is independent and not affiliated with the NBA or ESPN. The feed is undocumented and may become unavailable. No fabricated scores, injuries or probabilities are substituted when coverage fails. Historical model quality is not a guarantee of future accuracy.
