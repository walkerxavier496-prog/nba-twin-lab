04 / TRANSPARENCY

How this model works

Explainable estimates, not invented certainty.

01

Opponent-adjusted team strength

Elo moves after every final score. Beating a strong opponent counts more than beating a weak one; point margin adjusts the update. Ratings regress toward average between seasons.

02

Every possession is played

Each simulated possession can end in a turnover, free throws, a 2 or 3, a miss, or an offensive rebound. Validated prior-season pace and team rates shape those events; current results, rest, home court and Elo set the matchup expectation.

03

Real-world verification

Walk-forward evaluation recreates the forecast before each finished game using only earlier results. Accuracy, Brier score, calibration and projected-score error are compared with the current Elo model.

04

Limits we won't hide

Injuries, confirmed lineups, player minutes, trades, travel and betting odds are not included. Prior-season style can miss offseason changes, and the current held-out sample is still small.

Data provenance: Public, unofficial ESPN NBA scoreboard. This site is independent and not affiliated with the NBA or ESPN. The feed is undocumented and may become unavailable. No fabricated scores, injuries or probabilities are substituted when coverage fails. Historical model quality is not a guarantee of future accuracy.
