## Team marks

The 30 club logo URLs in `assets/team-media.js` were copied from the verified schedule and results metadata in `data/games.json`; ESPN's public image URLs typically follow `https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/nba/500/scoreboard/<team>.png`. No team logo has been altered or redrawn. Team names and marks remain the intellectual property of their owners. They are shown only to identify the teams under discussion. Missing images automatically fall back to readable club abbreviations.

## Player images

Conditional player-stat projections use NBA's CDN headshot URL with the **NBA player ID** already present in the frozen roster dataset: `https://cdn.nba.com/headshots/nba/latest/260x190/<NBA_PLAYER_ID>.png`. These images may be missing; the portrait component has a fallback. NBA.com headshots remain the property of their respective rights holders.

**IMPORTANT:** Public accessibility of an ESPN/NBA CDN asset does **not** by itself grant a republication, commercial or sublicensing right. Before commercialising the site or displaying advertising, the project owner should secure any rights needed for NBA/team marks and player headshots, replace those assets with authorised/licensed equivalents, and comply with applicable provider access terms. Do not imply that logos or portraits are provided under Wikimedia Creative Commons licences.

No displayed team marks, headshots or archival photography should be used to imply official endorsement, a current live game, or guaranteed game predictions.
