What we are testing

Calibrated Elo: Adjusts Elo's probability scale using only earlier seasons. Player: Adds estimated minutes and player impact from previous appearances. Team: Adds opponent-adjusted efficiency and back-to-back rest. Combined: Adds rotation continuity as well.

Player statuses are inferred, not verified injury reports. An added feature earns a place in live predictions only after robust, repeatable out-of-sample improvement.
