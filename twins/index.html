03 / How similarity works

Each metric becomes a percentile within its own NBA season. Player fingerprints are minutes-weighted averages. The advanced model balances scoring, playmaking, rebounding, defence, size and impact groups.

Lower distance = closer statistical style, not greater ability. Missing metrics are never invented; low-coverage comparisons are excluded.
